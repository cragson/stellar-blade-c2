## Trademarks and game data

Stellar Blade is a trademark of its owners. This is an unofficial fan project and is not
affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by SHIFT UP Corporation or Sony Interactive
Entertainment. `items.js`, `gamedata.js` and `chapters.js` contain identifiers from the game's data
tables and level files (with English names, short descriptions and object positions), `intel.js`
the names and parameters of the game's developer console commands and the developers' command sets
(with English names where the game has them), and `world.js` the positions of world objects, item pickups,
enemy and character spawns and zone triggers from the level files, with their contents, names, passcodes,
side quest steps and area names from the data tables, and each world's shape drawn from the level files'
navigation mesh and geometry (as a small mask image, not the game's artwork); `codex-data.js` enemy, fish,
manual and credits data, `builds.js` moves, inputs, gear and stat formulas, `dialogue.js` the structure and
conditions of NPC conversations (string keys only, no dialogue text) and `ai.js` enemy behaviour trees and
attacks. Longer texts (descriptions, dialogue lines, manual pages) are not included: they come from the
player's own copy of the game through the pack. They are used only so players can recognise, look up and
change what is in their own save files.
